I. QUESTION AND SCOPE:
    Question: What are the 6 types of AI- Descrpitive AI, Diagnostic AI, Predictive AI, Prescriptive AI, Genrative AI and Agentic AI?
    Scope: This research analyzes the definitions, practical examples, advantages, and disadvantges of the 6 types of AI.It aslo helps understand the 6 types of AI by giving Real-World examples from organisations supported by reliable references. The research also helps determine which is the appropriate AI type and applies it to three business problems faced by ShopSmart AI using decision guide. And also recommends which type of Ai should be implemented first in ShopSmart AI considering its potential benefits, limitations and business requirements.

II. FINDINGS:

1.  Descriptive Ai 
    - Definition: answers the question "what happened ?"
    - Example: 
    i. Google Analytics provides e-commerce reports that helps online businesses to summarize their current and past sales performances, which can help online stores determine which of their top 3 or top 5 best selling products, at what time do they have the most sales, is there any particular event whuch causes the high sales (If it is an online company which sells lamps custom diyas then its sales will be high during the festival season), which place are most of their orders from, determine their most loyal customer ( can give a thank you gift).
    (https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/collection/ga4/ecommerce)
    ii. Google Analytics Data API allows business to access website and retrieve application performance data.an online platform can use it to count the number of active users per day, identify the most frequently used wepage, compare when the website traffic is high, and showing user engagement and performance, with this it can help the buisiness montior the website performance and understand user activity.
    (https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/reporting/data/v1?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    - Advantages: It can summarize all the necessary details which can help improve the business of websites, stores etc.
    - Disadvantages: It cannot explain the occurance of any particular incident.

2. Diagnostic AI
    - Definition: Answers the question "Why it happened ?"
    - Example:
    i. Amazon reasearchers developed a system called Revenue Loss Monitor (RLM) which can help the online sellers to determine the factors causing the loss of revenue. It mainly considers factors susch as pricing, advertising, the product description and inventory stocks, and distinguishes these factors it can find out which is contributing in the loss of the revenue.
    (https://www.amazon.science/publications/beyond-correlation-deploying-causal-ai-to-attribute-e-commerce-sales-loss-at-scale?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    ii. Microsoft Power BI provides a decomposition tree which can allow users to breakdown the metric across different dimensions and investigate the cause of the problem. It helps business owners to  investigate the factors which are responsible fro the business problem and conduct root cause analysis.
    (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/visuals/power-bi-visualization-decomposition-tree?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    - Advnatages:Helps determine why the particular incident happened and what caused it.
    - Disadvantages:It can determine the cause by reading patterns in the data.

3. Predictive AI
    - Definition:Answers the question "what MAY happen next ?".
    - Example:
    i. Amazon uses ml models to predict the future customer demands for products which will be determined using the historical sales, patterns,price ranges , like before a huge sale the system can predict which products will be on higher demand and can help paln the inventory of those products and keep stocks of those products nearby to the customers 
    (https://www.amazon.science/latest-news/solving-some-of-the-largest-most-complex-operations-problems?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    ii. Netflix uses a recommendation system that can predict which movie or series the user may like based on what the user's history, ratings, interactions, etc.
    (https://help.netflix.com/en/node/100639?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    - Advnatages: It helps the business prepare for future events or incidents based on the predictions.
    - Disadvantages:The predictied data may not be correct, the predictions maybe wrong if the data is incomplete or the conditions change.

4. Prescriptive AI
    - Definition: Answers the question "What CAN BE DONE ?".
    - Example:
    i. United Parcel Services (UPS) uses a system called ORION (On-Road Integrated Optimization Navigation) to recommend its driver efficient driving routes. It analyses the delivery requirements such as delivery date, the type of product etc, to determine suitable routes to helps with delivery efficiency. By using ORION the drivers can save driving in unnecessary routes, save fuel and time.
    (https://www.bsr.org/en/case-studies/center-for-technology-and-sustainability-orion-technology-ups?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    ii. AWS Compute optimizer analyses the available data and recommends the correct cloud resource configuration that can be used in the business. It analyses the CPU, memory, and other utilisation metrics; identifies resources that are highly capable or under capable for the business.
    (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/compute-optimizer/latest/ug/what-is-compute-optimizer.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    - Advnatages:Recommands actions based on the predictions, data constraints, end goal.
    - Disadvantages:The recommandations are mostly based on assumptions.

It will analyse the data given by the user campare the product and matches it to the user's requirements and recommends options.It also uses more advanced agentic models for adding proiducts top the shoppingb cart removing products from the shopping cart etc.

    - Disadvantages: It needs to be monitoried for any chyanges in the given data and whether it is using the right tools or models, and requires permissions, if it is a multi step process sometimes it needs human intercations as well.

III. DECISION GUIDE:
    - If the business needs to summarize the past results, collect the past data, collect the problematic data or anything that should be counted then Descriptive AI can be used.
    -  If the business needs to investigate causes, analyzes the data to find the root of problems etc; Diagnostic AI can be used.
    - Predicting future outcomes using the data, then Predictive AI can be used.
    - If any recommendation actions are required Prescriptive AI can be used.
    - Any new content(eg: paragraphs, videos, audios, and images) needs to be generated then Generative AI can be used.
    - If businesses needs multiple step tasks carried out using tools, Agentic AI can be used.

IV. APPLICATION TO SHOPSMART AI:
    1. Use descrpitive AI to analyze weekly sales and the best-selling products in a whole week.
    2. Use predictive AI to estimate which products may ahve high demand based on the prior details and analyzed data.
    3. Prescriptive AI to recommends stock replenishment and to recommend the customers which products they may like based on their previous order.
    4. Use Generative AI to answer questions from customers in the chatbot in a way the customers understandable language.

V. RECOMMENDATIONS:
    Implement Predictive AI first: Predictive AI would be implemented first as ShopSmart AI is an online store, predictive AI can be used to predict the customers demand which can help the business by predicting which products to keep in stock more.

VI. Limitations:

    2. This research does not include the financial and tecnical comparisons of the differen types of ai approaches. Implementing AI technologies require investment softwares, computer resource, data preparations, and maintainence. The cost may wary based on the solution, business requirements, and organizataion's objective.
    3. Implementing Predictive AI first in ShopSmart AI for predicting customer demand depends on the availablity of reliable historical sales data. If the business is new then the lack of historical data would not help with implementing predictive AI first, and even if the sales data is not reliables, incomplete, or insufficient, the prediction may not be correct.
