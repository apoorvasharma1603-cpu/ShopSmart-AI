1.  Descriptive Ai 
    - Definition:answers the question "what happened ?"
    - Example: Google Analytics provides e-commerce reports that helps online businesses to summarize their curetrent and past sales performances, which can help online stores determine which of their top 3 or top 5 best selling products, at what time do they have the most sales, is there any particular event whuch causes the high sales (If it is an online company which sells lamps custom diyas then its sales will be high during the festival season), which place are most of their orders from, determine their most loyal customer ( can give a thank you gift).
    (https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/collection/ga4/ecommerce)
    - Advantages: It can summarize all the necessary details which can help improve the business of websites, stores etc.
    - Disadvantages: It cannot explain the occurance of any particular incident.

2. Diagnostic AI
    - Definition: Answers the question "Why it happened ?"
    - Example: Anmazon reasearchers developed a system called Revenue Loss Monitor (RLM) which can help the online sellers to determine the factors causing the loss of revenue. It mainly considers factors susch as pricing, advertising, the product description and inventory stocks, and distinguishes these factors it can find out which is contributing in the loss of the revenue.
    (https://www.amazon.science/publications/beyond-correlation-deploying-causal-ai-to-attribute-e-commerce-sales-loss-at-scale?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    - Advnatages:Helps determine why the particular incident happened and what caused it.
    - Disadvantages:It can determine the cause by reading patterns in the data.

3. Predictive AI
    - Definition:Answers the question "what MAY happen next ?".
    - Example:Amazon uses ml models to predict the future customer demands for products which will be determined using the historical sales, patterns,price ranges , like before a huge sale the system can predict which products will be on higher demand and can help paln the inventory of those products and keep stocks of those products nearby to the customers 
    (https://www.amazon.science/latest-news/solving-some-of-the-largest-most-complex-operations-problems?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    - Advnatages: It helps the business prepare for future events or incidents based on the predictions.
    - Disadvantages:The predictied data may not be correct, the predictions maybe wrong if the data is incomplete or the conditions change.

4. Prescriptive AI
    - Definition: Answers the question "What CAN BE DONE ?".
    - Example: United Parcel Services (UPS) uses a system called ORION (On-Road Integrated Optimization Navigation) to recommend its driver efficient driving routes. It analyses the delivery requirements such as delivery date, the type of product etc, to determine suitable routes to helps with delivery efficiency. By using ORION the drivers can save driving in unnecessary routes, save fuel and time.
    (https://www.bsr.org/en/case-studies/center-for-technology-and-sustainability-orion-technology-ups?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    - Advnatages:Recommands actions based on the predictions, data constraints, end goal.
    - Disadvantages:The recommandations are mostly based on assumptions. 

5. Generative AI
    - Definition: It generates answers to the user like text paragraphs, images, audio, videos etc.
    - Example: Lenovo uses and AI- enhanced customer support chat service using llm to provide faster and more accurate reponses to the customer queries. It supports multi lingual conversations and helps the support agents solve the customer queries easily.
     (https://news.lenovo.com/lpl-unlocks-value-of-generative-ai-in-customer-support/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    - Advnatages: Generates content in human language, likle answering queries, generating an image or an audio file based on the user requirements. 
    - Disadvantages:it cxan generate inaccurate content or hallucinate the data which is required for generating the content which is why it should be checked for accuracy.

It will analyse the data given by the user campare the product and matches it to the user's requirements and recommends options.It also uses more advanced agentic models for adding proiducts top the shoppingb cart removing products from the shopping cart etc.

    - Disadvantages: It needs to be monitoried for any chyanges in the given data and whether it is using the right tools or models, and requires permissions, if it is a multi step process sometimes it needs human intercations as well.
